MongoDB Advanced Assignment
Task 6 - Backup and Restore Commands
============================================================


1. BACKUP company_advanced

mongodump --db company_advanced --archive=company_advanced.archive --gzip


2. RESTORE INTO A NEW DATABASE

mongorestore --archive=company_advanced.archive --gzip --nsFrom="company_advanced.*" --nsTo="company_advanced_restore.*"


3. CONNECT TO THE RESTORED DATABASE

mongosh

use company_advanced_restore


4. VERIFY COLLECTIONS

show collections


5. VERIFY EMPLOYEE COUNT

db.employees.countDocuments()


6. VERIFY DEPARTMENT COUNT

db.departments.countDocuments()


7. VERIFY SESSION COUNT

db.sessions.countDocuments()


8. VERIFY AN IMPORTANT RESTORED EMPLOYEE

db.employees.findOne({ name: "Alice" })


9. VERIFY ALL RESTORED EMPLOYEES

db.employees.find().sort({ _id: 1 }).forEach(printjson)


10. VERIFY ALL RESTORED DEPARTMENTS

db.departments.find().sort({ _id: 1 }).forEach(printjson)


============================================================
Expected important result:
employees count = 4
departments count = 2

The sessions count may depend on whether the TTL document
has already expired when the backup is performed.
